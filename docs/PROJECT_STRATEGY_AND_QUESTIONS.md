# Project Strategy, Enterprise Fleet Architecture & Submission Roadmap

This document establishes the strategic roadmap, multi-agent fleet architecture, infrastructure design, client experience paradigms, and hackathon submission deliverables for the **Logistics Hackathon** under the **Fortified Enterprise Fleet** track.

* **Target Code Repository**: [`https://github.com/CostaCloudSA/all-things-logistics`](https://github.com/CostaCloudSA/all-things-logistics)

---

## 1. Commercial SaaS Vision & Hackathon Track Alignment

### 1.1 Fortified Enterprise Fleet Track Alignment
The platform is designed as an institutional, white-labeled B2B SaaS cross-border compliance engine for logistics carriers, customs brokerages, and 3PLs across the Americas. It delivers:
- **Zero-Trust Security**: Dedicated Agent Identities and least-privilege IAM per domain.
- **Model Armor**: Local Gemma PII sanitization and deterministic tariff grounding.
- **Asynchronous Scalability**: Cloud Run + Pub/Sub runtime for bulk manifest processing.
- **Full Observability**: OpenTelemetry-instrumented reasoning and audit traces.

## 3. The "Zero-Typing" Mobile Interaction Model

Field sales reps, warehouse dock operators, and drivers need rapid data entry without typing complex technical jargon:

1. **Camera Vision OCR Document Ingestion**:
   - Ingests commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, or shipping labels via camera scan.
   - Automatically populates item names, quantities, weights, origins, and buyer/seller details without manual typing.
2. **Smart Contextual Tap Buttons (Chips)**:
   - Dynamic, single-tap interactive selection chips generated by Gemini 3.7 Flash:
     - *Product Subheading*: `[ 🍗 Whole Frozen ]` `[ 🍗 Cut Pieces / Offal ]` `[ 🍗 Deboned Breast ]`
     - *Valuation Basis*: `[ 💲 FOB Price ]` `[ 💲 CIF Price ]` `[ 💲 De Minimis Exempt ]`
3. **Generative Voice-to-Trade Parser**:
   - Sales reps press a single microphone button to speak naturally in Spanish, English, or Portuguese. AI extracts trade variables instantly.

## 4. Dynamic Country-Adaptive Health & Regulatory Routing Engine

The regulatory clearance workflow **dynamically adapts based on both Sender (Origin ISO) and Receiver (Destination ISO)** across all 35 sovereign nations in the Americas.

| Target Destination | Customs Authority | Primary Health & Sanitary Authorities | Golden Documents Generated |

| **United States (`US`)** | U.S. CBP | • **USDA / APHIS**: Animal Health Permit<br>• **USDA / FSIS**: Foreign Est. Inspection<br>• **FDA**: Prior Notice (PN) Submission | • CBP Form 7501 (Entry Summary)<br>• CBP Form 3461 (Immediate Delivery)<br>• US-Colombia TPA Origin Cert. |
| **Mexico (`MX`)** | ANAM / SAT | • **SENASICA (SADER)**: Certificado Zoosanitario de Importación<br>• **COFEPRIS**: Permiso Sanitario | • Pedimento Aduanal 3.0<br>• CFDI con Complemento Carta Porte<br>• Certificado SENASICA |
