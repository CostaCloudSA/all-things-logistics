# Logistics SME Field Interview Guide: 45+ High-Value Operational Questions

> **Interview Objective**: Fact-check operational assumptions with a veteran multinational logistics professional (decades of experience in freight forwarding, naval shipping, drayage, and cross-border customs across the Americas).
> **Guiding Principle**: Avoid pitching technology solutions. Focus exclusively on operational realities, bottlenecks, physical handoffs, timing of documentation, and integration with existing paper/EDI legacy workflows.

---

## 📋 Section A: Pre-Shipment Planning & Booking Reality

1. **Information Velocity vs. Cargo Velocity**: At what exact point in the lifecycle does the commercial invoice and packing list become "final"? How frequently do weights, counts, or values change *after* the container is already sealed at the origin warehouse?
2. **Incoterm Nuances in the Field**: On paper, contracts say FOB or CIF. In practice, who actually pays the unexpected terminal handling charges (THC), chassis split fees, or phytosanitary inspection fees when cargo arrives at the port?
3. **Carrier Booking Confirmations**: What are the most common reasons ocean/air bookings get rolled or delayed at origin (e.g., equipment shortages, missing export permits, misdeclared cargo)?
4. **Origin Pre-Clearance**: For agricultural and refrigerated goods (e.g., poultry from Colombia or avocados from Mexico), how much inspection or testing occurs at origin before the carrier accepts the reefer?
5. **Commercial Invoice Ambiguities**: When a shipper hands you an invoice that just says "Spare parts" or "Frozen meat cuts", what is your exact operational procedure to track down the true technical description?
6. **Booking Amendments**: How many times is a shipping instruction or Bill of Lading revised before vessel departure? Who bears the cost and delay of late amendments?

---

## 📑 Section B: Commercial Documentation, Invoicing & Handoffs

7. **The Paper vs. Digital Reality**: Despite EDI and portals, how much physical paper (printed stamping, original bills of lading, physical sanitary certificates) is still required to move a box through ports in Latin America vs. the US?
8. **Original Bill of Lading (OBL) Bottlenecks**: How often are shipments physically sitting in port unable to be cleared simply because the bank or courier hasn't released the physical original Bill of Lading?
9. **Multi-Item Manifests**: When a 40ft container contains 50 different SKUs with different materials and origins, how do brokers handle the classification workload without holding up the shipment?
10. **Sanitary Certificate Timing**: For USDA/SENASICA/MAPA shipments, does the sanitary certificate travel physically with the driver/vessel, or is it transmitted electronically ahead of time? What happens if the ink seal is slightly smudged?
11. **Weight & Cube Verification (VGM)**: How is Verified Gross Mass (VGM) verified in practice at origin ports? What happens when the scale weight at the port gate differs from the packing list weight?
12. **Dangerous Goods (DG / IMO)**: For cargo with lithium batteries or chemicals, what are the handoff failure points between shipper declarations and vessel stowage acceptance?
13. **Language Barriers**: How are descriptions translated between Spanish/Portuguese and English during customs filings? Who is legally liable if a translation error causes an incorrect HS code?

---

## 🚢 Section C: Ports, Terminals & Border Crossing Realities

14. **Port Congestion & Terminal Gate In**: What is the average wait time for drayage trucks outside major container terminals (e.g., Miami, Houston, Manzanillo, Santos, Cartagena)?
15. **Reefer Monitoring**: For temperature-controlled cargo (poultry, produce, pharmaceuticals), who monitors the temperature log while the container sits in the terminal yard? What happens during a power plug unplug incident?
16. **Customs "Red Light" Physical Inspections**: When a container is pulled for secondary or intensive customs inspection (e.g., CBP VACIS X-ray, USDA tail-gate inspection, or Mexican SAT Reconocimiento Aduanero), what is the real-world timeline and cost impact?
17. **Cross-Border Drayage & Transfer (US-Mexico Border)**: At crossings like Nuevo Laredo/Laredo or Otay Mesa, explain the exact handoff between the Mexican carrier, the transfer/drayage driver, the Mexican customs broker, the US customs broker, and the US long-haul carrier. Where do delays happen most?
18. **Chassis Availability**: In US and Latin American ports, how severe is the chassis shortage, and who manages chassis split fees or roadability repairs?
19. **Customs Hold Notification Timing**: How does a logistics coordinator find out that a container has a customs hold (e.g., FDA hold, USDA hold, PGA hold)? Is it automated EDI, an email, or does the trucker find out at the gate?
20. **Transshipment Hub Vulnerabilities**: For cargo transshipping through Panama, Kingston, or Cartagena, what are the primary risks of documentation desynchronization between feeder vessels?

---

## 🏛️ Section D: Customs Brokerage, Valuation & Regulatory Audits

21. **The Broker-Importer Relationship**: How much autonomy does a licensed customs broker have to assign an HS code versus requiring explicit sign-off from the importer of record?
22. **Tariff Rate Surprises & Anti-Dumping**: How often do importers discover *after* arrival that their goods are subject to unexpected Section 301 tariffs, anti-dumping duties (ADD), or countervailing duties (CVD)?
23. **De Minimis Exploitation & Scrutiny**: With increasing customs scrutiny on Section 321 ($800 US) and $50 Latin American de minimis parcels, how are carriers handling compliance risk on consolidated manifest shipments?
24. **Customs Bonds & Liability**: When CBP or SAT levies a penalty for misclassification or undervaluation, how is liability shared between the shipper, freight forwarder, and customs broker?
25. **Post-Summary Corrections (PSC) & Amendments**: How frequently do companies file corrections or reconciliations after goods have already cleared customs? What triggers these audits?
26. **Free Trade Agreement (FTA) Certification**: In practice, how are USMCA / CAFTA-DR / Colombia TPA certificates of origin validated? How often are claims rejected due to missing regional value content (RVC) proof?
27. **Sanitary Sampling Protocols**: When agricultural inspectors take physical samples for lab testing, where is the cargo stored, and who pays the refrigerated warehouse storage fees while waiting for lab results?
28. **Valuation Methodologies**: For inter-company transfer pricing or shipments between related parties, how do brokers substantiate the declared transaction value to customs inspectors?

---

## ⏳ Section E: Demurrage, Detention, Free Time & Billing

29. **The Free Time Clock**: Explain the exact difference and operational nightmare between "Demurrage" (inside terminal) and "Detention/Per Diem" (outside terminal). When does the free time clock start ticking, and why is it so hard to track?
30. **Uncontrollable Demurrage**: What happens when customs puts an intensive hold on a container for 10 days, but ocean line free time is only 4 days? Does the ocean carrier waive demurrage, or does the importer get billed thousands of dollars?
31. **Empty Container Return Logistics**: Why is returning an empty container to the terminal often as difficult as picking up a full one (e.g., dual transactions, terminal gate closures, appointment systems)?
32. **Driver Detention Pay**: When a trucker waits 5 hours at a warehouse dock or port gate, how is driver detention tracked, verified, and billed back to the customer?
33. **Accessorial Charges Explosion**: What percentage of freight invoices contain unexpected accessorials (chassis split, storage, pre-pull, dry run, hazmat surcharge, scale fees)? How are these validated against reality?
34. **Carrier Invoicing Accuracy**: How often are freight forwarders and shippers overbilled on carrier invoices? How much manual labor is spent auditing freight bills?
35. **Cargo Insurance & Claim Realities**: When cargo is damaged or spoiled during a customs delay, what is the actual claim settlement rate under ocean carrier limitations ($500 per package COGSA)?

---

## 💻 Section F: Legacy Systems, EDI/API Realities & Field Workers

36. **Core Systems in Use**: What Transport Management Systems (TMS), Freight Forwarding Systems (e.g., CargoWise, Magaya, Descartes, BluJay), and Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) do you see most frequently across mid-size and enterprise logistics operators?
37. **EDI 214, 310, 753 Realities**: How reliable are legacy EDI milestone messages? Why are status updates like "Vessel Arrived" or "Customs Released" often delayed by 12 to 24 hours in EDI pipelines?
38. **The WhatsApp / Email Phenomenon**: How much of day-to-day operations and exception handling still happens over WhatsApp group chats, mobile phone calls, and unread email threads rather than inside official enterprise software?
39. **The "Deskless" Field Worker**: What tools do yard workers, dispatchers, and warehouse receiving staff actually use on their shifts? Why do complex desktop software portals fail on the warehouse floor?
40. **Spreadsheet Dependency**: What critical operational data is still being tracked in offline Excel spreadsheets because the company's enterprise software is too rigid to accommodate it?
41. **Driver Communication**: How do dispatchers communicate changes in route, port appointments, or pickup numbers to independent owner-operators who may not speak English fluently?
42. **Data Silos between Departments**: Where is the biggest disconnect inside a logistics company—between Sales and Operations, Operations and Customs, or Customs and Billing?

---

## 🪄 Section G: "Magic Wand" AI Wishlist Open Questions

43. **The Single Worst Daily Pain Point**: *"If you had a magic wand and could eliminate one single repetitive task, paperwork bottleneck, or communication headache from your daily operations forever, what would it be and why?"*
44. **The Zero-Friction Hand-off**: *"If an intelligent system could silently watch your shipments and take one automatic action without you having to click a button or type on a keyboard, what action would save you the most money or stress?"*
45. **The Unfair Advantage**: *"If you could instantly know one piece of hidden information 48 hours before anyone else in the supply chain (e.g., customs hold probability, terminal gate congestion, carrier invoice discrepancies, sanitary inspection triggers), which one would give your company the ultimate competitive advantage?"*
46. **The Ideal Mobile Tool for Field Staff**: *"If you could design a tool that a driver, warehouse receiver, or port dispatcher could operate purely with voice and camera in 10 seconds without touching a keyboard, what must that tool display or calculate on the spot?"*
47. **The Ultimate Regulatory Safety Net**: *"What kind of automated monitoring would give you 100% confidence that none of your shipments will ever be seized, penalized, or delayed by customs authorities across any country in the Americas?"*
