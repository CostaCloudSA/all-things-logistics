## 🏛️ System Highlights & Architectural Pillars

1. **AI Engine**: **Gemini 3.7 Flash** (released August 13) for high-speed multi-agent reasoning, multimodal OCR, classification, and dynamic Golden Document generation.
2. **Security & Model Armor**: Local **Gemma** model on edge/device for PII/confidential commercial data scrubbing (+0.2 Bonus Points) + deterministic BigQuery tariff lookup gates + **Ed25519 Asymmetric Cryptographic Manifest Seals**.
3. **Cross-Tenant A2A Federation**: Decentralized **Agent-to-Agent (A2A) B2B Handshakes** allowing independent white-labeled instances to exchange manifests with unbroken W3C distributed trace propagation (`traceparent`).
4. **Field Inspector Encrypted QR Code**: Offline-scannable QR seal for roadside weigh stations, highway police, and border checkpoints (verifying axle weights and sanitary stamps in $<1$s).
5. **The Conglomerate Multiplier**: Multi-tenant operational mesh enabling Shippers, 3PLs, Motor Carriers, and Terminals to eliminate 100% of duplicate data entry.
6. **White-Labeling & Sovereign Data Isolation**: Dynamic in-app persona switching across 3 enterprise logistics verticals:
   - **Campabadal Global Logistics** (`#0284C7` Electric Blue): Multinational 3PL Freight Forwarder.
   - **Transportes Tomas** (`#DC2626` Vibrant Red): Cross-Border Drayage & Transload Motor Carrier.
   - **Agroexport Costa Rica** (`#059669` Emerald Green): Perishables Cold-Chain Shipper.
7. **Cross-Platform Client**: **Flutter** mobile-first Zero-Typing UX (Camera Vision OCR + Guided Smart Chips + Axle Weight Visualizer + Night-Watch Status Card + Field Inspector QR Card).
8. **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**: **Terraform** managing Multi-Region Cloud Run, Cloud Pub/Sub, IAM Zero-Trust Service Accounts, and BigQuery Domain-Driven Data Mesh.
9. **Observability**: **OpenTelemetry** end-to-end reasoning chains and immutable compliance audit logs.
