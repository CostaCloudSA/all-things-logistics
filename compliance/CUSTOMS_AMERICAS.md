# Customs Agencies & Regulatory Rules Across the Americas

This document provides a comprehensive regulatory directory and operational rulebook for customs authorities across **North America, Central America, South America, and the Caribbean**. It serves as the authoritative data reference for international trade compliance, customs automated clearance logic, documentation validation, and cross-border logistics workflow engines in the **Logistics Hackathon** system.

---

## Regional Trade Agreements Summary

| Agreement | Full Name | Key Member States | Key Regulatory Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **USMCA / T-MEC / CUSMA** | United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement | USA, CAN, MEX | Strict Rules of Origin, digital trade clearance, duty-free threshold ($800 De Minimis in USA). |
| **MERCOSUR** | Mercado Común del Sur | ARG, BRA, PRY, URY, BOL | Common External Tariff (AEC), SINTIA transport tracking system, unified transit manifests. |
| **Pacific Alliance** | Alianza del Pacífico | CHL, COL, MEX, PER | Interoperable Single Windows (VUCE), 92% instant tariff elimination on intra-regional trade. |
| **CAFTA-DR** | Dominican Republic-Central America FTA | USA, CRI, SLV, GTM, HND, NIC, DOM | Central American Single Market (DUCA manifest), harmonized customs clearance protocols. |
| **CARICOM** | Caribbean Community | JAM, TTO, BRB, BHS, GUY, SUR, etc. | Common External Tariff (CET), ASYCUDA World electronic manifest processing. |

## 1. North America

### 1.1 United States of America (`US`)
- **Customs Authority**: U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) / ACE Secure Data Portal
- **Key Import Declarations**: Entry Summary (CBP Form 7501), Manifest (CBP Form 3461), Importer Security Filing (ISF / 10+2 for ocean shipments).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$800 USD** (Section 321 - Duty and tax-free entry for low-value shipments).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading (BOL) / Air Waybill (AWB), Packing List, USMCA Certificate of Origin (if applicable).
- **Special Regulatory Agencies**: FDA (Food & Drugs), USDA/APHIS (Agriculture), EPA (Environment), FCC (Electronics).

### 1.2 Canada (`CA`)
- **Customs Authority**: Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) / Agence des services frontaliers du Canada
- **Primary Electronic System**: Assessment and Revenue Management (CARM) / eManifest
- **Key Import Declarations**: B3-3 Canada Customs Coding Form, Advanced Commercial Information (ACI).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$20 CAD** for taxes/duties ($150 CAD duty-free threshold under CUSMA for courier shipments from US/MX).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Canada Customs Invoice (CCI) or Commercial Invoice with required fields, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin.

### 1.3 Mexico (`MX`)
- **Customs Authority**: Agencia Nacional de Aduanas de México (ANAM) / Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Ventanilla Única de Comercio Exterior Mexicana (VUCEM)
- **Key Import Declarations**: Pedimento Aduanal (Official customs entry declaration processed by registered Customs Agent / Agente Aduanal).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$50 USD** (Duty and VAT exempt for express courier); $117 USD duty-free under T-MEC.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Pedimento, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, COFEPRIS / SAGARPA permits (if applicable), Official Mexican Standards (NOM) Compliance Certificates, Digital Transport Invoice (CFDI con Complemento Carta Porte).

## 2. Central America

### 2.1 Costa Rica (`CR`)
- **Customs Authority**: Servicio Nacional de Aduanas (Ministerio de Hacienda)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema de Información Aduanera TICA (Tecnología de Información para el Control Aduanero)
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración Única Centroamericana (DUCA) - DUCA-F (intra-CA FTA), DUCA-D (extra-regional imports), DUCA-T (international transit).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUCA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, PROCOMER / MAG permits for food/agriculture.

### 2.2 Panama (`PA`)
- **Customs Authority**: Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema Integrado de Gestión Aduanera (SIGA)
- **Special Status**: Colon Free Zone (ZLC) - Major re-export logistics hub in the Western Hemisphere.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Declaración de Mercancías, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/AWB, Certificate of Origin, AUPSA / MIDA permits for agri-food.

### 2.3 Guatemala (`GT`)
- **Customs Authority**: Superintendencia de Administración Tributaria (SAT - Intendencia de Aduanas)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Declaraguate / Sistema Aduanero SAUQ
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUCA (DUCA-D / DUCA-F / DUCA-T).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUCA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, SAT registration (NIT), MAGA permits.

### 2.4 El Salvador (`SV`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección General de Aduanas (Ministerio de Hacienda)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SIDUNEA World (ASYCUDA World)
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUCA.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUCA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, MAG / MINSAL permits.

### 2.5 Honduras (`HN`)
- **Customs Authority**: Administración Aduanera de Honduras (Aduanas Honduras)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SARAH (Sistema Automatizado de Rentas de Honduras) / ASYCUDA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUCA.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUCA, Commercial Invoice, Transport Document, ARSA health registrations.

### 2.6 Nicaragua (`NI`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección General de Servicios Aduaneros (DGA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SIDUNEA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUCA.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUCA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, IPSA permits.

### 2.7 Belize (`BZ`)
- **Customs Authority**: Belize Customs and Excise Department
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: Customs Single Administrative Document (C200).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C200 Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, BAHA (Belize Agricultural Health Authority) permits.

## 3. South America

### 3.1 Brazil (`BR`)
- **Customs Authority**: Secretaria da Receita Federal do Brasil (RFB)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Siscomex (Sistema Integrado de Comércio Exterior) / Portal Único Siscomex
- **Key Import Declarations**: Declaração Única de Importação (DUImp) / Declaração de Importação (DI).
- **De Minimis / Remessa Conforme**: **$50 USD** threshold for cross-border e-commerce under the "Remessa Conforme" tax program (subject to ICMS tax).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUImp/DI, Commercial Invoice (Fatura Comercial), Knowledge of Transport (Conhecimento de Carga - Bill of Lading/AWB), Packing List (Romaneio de Carga), ANVISA/MAPA regulatory licenses.

### 3.2 Argentina (`AR`)
- **Customs Authority**: Agencia de Recaudación y Control Aduanero (ARCA) - Dirección General de Aduanas (DGA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema Informático Malvina (SIM) / SIRA (Sistema de Importaciones de la República Argentina)
- **Key Import Declarations**: Declaración Jurada SIM, Certificado de Tipificación.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: SIM Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Packing List, ANMAT/SENASA sanitary permits.

### 3.3 Colombia (`CO`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN)
- **Primary Electronic System**: Ventanilla Única de Comercio Exterior (VUCE) / SYGA
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración de Importación (Formulario 500).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$200 USD** (Duty-free entry under US-Colombia FTA for qualifying express shipments).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Declaración de Importación, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/AWB, Certificate of Origin, INVIMA / ICA certificates.

### 3.4 Chile (`CL`)
- **Customs Authority**: Servicio Nacional de Aduanas
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema Sicex-Chile (Sistema de Importaciones y Exportaciones)
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración de Ingreso (DIN).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$41 USD** (Duty/tax exempt threshold).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DIN, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (Chile has FTAs with >60 countries), SAG/ISP health certificates.

### 3.5 Peru (`PE`)
- **Customs Authority**: Superintendencia Nacional de Aduanas y de Administración Tributaria (SUNAT - Aduanas)
- **Primary Electronic System**: VUCE Perú / Sistema de Despacho Aduanero (SDA)
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración Única de Aduanas (DUA) / Declaración de Mercancías.
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$200 USD** (Duty and VAT exempt for express courier imports).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, DIGESA / SENASA permits.

### 3.6 Ecuador (`EC`)
- **Customs Authority**: Servicio Nacional de Aduana del Ecuador (SENAE)
- **Primary Electronic System**: ECUAPASS
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración Aduanera de Importación (DAI).
- **De Minimis / Courier Category 4x4**: **$400 USD / 4 kg** (Duty-free for personal postal imports under Category B).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DAI, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, ARCSA / AGROCALIDAD inspection certificates.

### 3.7 Uruguay (`UY`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección Nacional de Aduanas (DNA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: LUCIA (Sistema de Información Aduanera)
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUA (Declaración Única de Aduanas).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (MERCOSUR Form), MGAP sanitary approval.

### 3.8 Paraguay (`PY`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección Nacional de Ingresos Tributarios (DNIT) - Gerencia General de Aduanas
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema SOFIA
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración Detallada de Importación.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Despacho de Importación, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, SENAVE / SENACSA permits.

### 3.9 Bolivia (`BO`)
- **Customs Authority**: Aduana Nacional de Bolivia (ANB)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SUMA (Sistema Único de Modernización Aduanera)
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración de Mercancías de Importación (DMI).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DMI, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, SENASAG sanitary permits.

### 3.10 Venezuela (`VE`)
- **Customs Authority**: Servicio Nacional Integrado de Administración Aduanera y Tributaria (SENIAT)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SIDUNEA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: Declaración Única de Aduanas.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Insai / Sancor health clearances.

### 3.11 Guyana (`GY`)
- **Customs Authority**: Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA) - Customs and Trade Administration
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: C72 Customs Declaration Form.
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C72 Form, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, CARICOM Origin Certificate (if applicable).

### 3.12 Suriname (`SR`)
- **Customs Authority**: Douane Suriname (Ministry of Finance)
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: Single Administrative Document (SAD).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: SAD Form, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Import License (if restricted goods).

## 4. Caribbean Nations

### 4.1 Dominican Republic (`DO`)
- **Customs Authority**: Dirección General de Aduanas (DGA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: SIGA (Sistema Integral de Gestión Aduanera)
- **Key Import Declaration**: DUA (Declaración Única Aduanera).
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$200 USD** (Duty and tax free under Decree 402-05 for express courier).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: DUA, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, CAFTA-DR Origin Certificate, DIGEMAPS/Ministerio de Agricultura permits.

### 4.2 Jamaica (`JM`)
- **Customs Authority**: Jamaica Customs Agency (JCA)
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Key Import Declaration**: C87 Entry Form.
- **De Minimis Threshold**: **$100 USD** (Duty-free threshold for non-commercial personal imports).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C87 Form, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/AWB, Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC).

### 4.3 Haiti (`HT`)
- **Customs Authority**: Administration Générale des Douanes (AGD)
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Déclaration de Douane, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading.

### 4.4 Trinidad and Tobago (`TT`)
- **Customs Authority**: Customs and Excise Division (Ministry of Finance)
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World / TTBizLink Single Window
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C75 Form, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, CARICOM Certificate of Origin.

### 4.5 Bahamas (`BS`)
- **Customs Authority**: Bahamas Customs Department
- **Primary Electronic System**: Click2Clear (Bahamas Electronic Single Window)
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C13 Import Entry Form, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading.

### 4.6 Barbados (`BB`)
- **Customs Authority**: Barbados Customs and Excise Department
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World
- **Mandatory Documentation**: C63 Customs Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading.

### 4.7 Cuba (`CU`)
- **Customs Authority**: Aduana General de la República de Cuba
- **Primary Electronic System**: Sistema Automatizado de Aduanas
- **Mandatory Documentation**: Declaración de Aduanas, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, MINCEX licenses.

### 4.8 OECS Nations (Eastern Caribbean)
*(Antigua & Barbuda `AG`, Dominica `DM`, Grenada `GD`, Saint Kitts & Nevis `KN`, Saint Lucia `LC`, Saint Vincent & the Grenadines `VC`)*
- **Primary Electronic System**: ASYCUDA World across all OECS member states.
- **Harmonized Framework**: CARICOM Common External Tariff (CET).
- **Mandatory Documentation**: ASYCUDA SAD Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/AWB, CARICOM Origin Certificate.

## 5. Customs Rules Implementation Checklist for Hackathon Logic Engine

When writing logic modules or schema validation routines for cross-border logistics:

1. **Valuation & Tariff Engine**:
   - Check destination ISO code against De Minimis thresholds ($800 US, $200 CO/DO/PE, $50 MX/BR, $41 CL).
   - Compute duty based on 6-digit Harmonized System (HS) Root Code + country-specific national subheadings (8 to 10 digits).

2. **Required Documents Matrix Generator**:
   - Dynamically prompt users for missing regulatory attachments based on commodity type (e.g., Food -> FDA/COFEPRIS/ANVISA/INVIMA; Medicines -> Health ministry permit; Animals/Plants -> Sanitary/Phytosanitary certificate).

3. **Trade Agreement Discount Router**:
   - Evaluate Origin ISO and Destination ISO to determine applicable FTAs (USMCA, MERCOSUR, CAFTA-DR, Pacific Alliance, CARICOM).
   - If origin certificate rule is met, automatically apply preferential zero or reduced duty rate.
